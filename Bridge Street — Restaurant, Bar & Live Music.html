Our Story

A destination on the banks of the Baakens.

Tucked away on the verdant banks of the Baakens River in Brickmakers Kloof, Bridge Street Bistro & Smokehouse has been a fixture of Gqeberha's Baakens Valley since 2011 — a craft brewery, restaurant and bar built around thin-crust wood-fired pizzas, smokehouse mains and great portions.

We use only the freshest ingredients, and the outdoor deck — set beneath beautiful old Acacia trees — seats up to 180 guests for everything from a weeknight pizza to a full corporate function. Friendly waitstaff, cold craft beer, and something happening on stage most weekends.

Bridge Street is proudly a brand of the In Good Company Hospitality Collective.

Live Music

Pub-style food, real bands, under the trees.

Join us in the lush Baakens Valley for pub-style food and live acoustic music. The outdoor seating space, set under the Acacia trees, comfortably hosts up to 180 guests — ideal for celebrating special occasions, corporate functions, or just a Friday night out.

  • Outdoor deck — up to 180 guests under mature Acacia trees.
